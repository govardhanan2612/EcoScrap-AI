# Unit Economics & Platform Sustainability Assessment

## 1. Executive Summary
Under the current informal regime, scrap collectors (Kabadiwalas and waste pickers) lose **20% to 35% of the true value** of electronic waste due to unstandardized weighing, lack of market rate awareness, and reliance on hazardous backyard extractors who only salvage rudimentary copper and crude gold while discarding valuable elements (Lithium, Neodymium, Tantalum, Cobalt). 

**E-Setu** transforms this dynamic by channeling clean, aggregated scrap into authorized CPCB-certified recyclers who monetize both recovered commodities and statutory **Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)** credits.

## 2. Unit Economics Comparison: Informal vs E-Setu Platform

| **Transportation & Handling Cost** | - ₹1,200 | - ₹1,400 (Recycler doorstep pickup shared) | - ₹200 |
| **Net Take-Home Pay for Collector** | **₹32,250** | **₹53,075** | **+ ₹20,825 net income (+64.5%)** |

### Why can Authorized Recyclers Pay So Much More?
1. **EPR Credit Revenue**: Recyclers receive ₹15 to ₹45 per kg in EPR certificate payouts from electronics brands (Samsung, HP, Dell) under CPCB targets.
2. **Superior Extraction Yield**: Modern hydrometallurgical refining achieves **98% precious metal recovery** (Gold, Silver, Palladium, Neodymium) vs only 20-30% in informal acid pits.
3. **Zero Fines & Compliance Safety**: Recyclers avoid pollution control penalties and shutdowns.

## 3. Platform Revenue & Financial Sustainability Model
How does E-Setu sustain its software, IoT weighbridge integrations, and vernacular field operations?

```
                                                                 │  Kabadiwalas & Citizens  │

                                                                 └──────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Zero Fee for Informal Collectors & Customers**:
   - Scrap dealers and waste pickers are **NEVER charged** any transaction fee or platform subscription. This ensures zero barrier to adoption.
2. **Recycler-Side Transaction Facilitation Fee (1.5% to 2.0%)**:
   - Authorized recyclers pay a 1.5% fee on verified material handovers for digitizing traceability, automating CPCB Form-6 manifest filings, and securing clean supply.
3. **EPR Compliance Traceability SaaS**:
   - Recyclers and Brand Producers subscribe to the CPCB-compliant audit dashboard for end-to-end chain-of-custody verification.
4. **Breakeven Metrics**:
   - At 150 MT/month of aggregated e-waste channeled through 4 industrial clusters (Pune, Mumbai, Nagpur, Nashik), platform revenue reaches **₹16.5 Lakhs/month**, fully funding technical infrastructure, field coordinators, and vernacular support centers.

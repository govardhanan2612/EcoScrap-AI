Getting In

EcoScrap AI has no passwords. Each role signs in a different way — pick yours below.

Customer

Enter your name, phone number, and pincode. That's it — you're in.

No PIN, no OTP, no waiting.

First move → open the Sell tab and scan something

Kabadiwala

Returning? Sign in with your phone + 4-digit PIN.

New? Register your yard, vehicle, and pincode — you're assigned PIN 1234 and your location is captured automatically.

First move → open Commodity Exchange to see live rates

Recycler

Enter your CPCB/SPCB registration number, e.g. CPCB/EPR-REC/2023/MH-0842.

First time, add your facility name and phone — after that, the number alone signs you back in.

First move → check your Incoming Lots queue
Role 1

Customer

You've got old wires, a dead phone battery, or a broken monitor. This is where it becomes cash at your doorstep.

Sell Tab
AI Scrap Scanner
Tap the camera card and photograph your item. It's identified instantly, given a confidence score, and graded A / B / C — a Grade A item pays a 1.1× bonus, Grade C pays 0.85×. Runs entirely on your device, so it works with zero signal.
Voice Selling
Tap the mic and just say what you have — "copper wire, two hundred grams." The material and weight fill in on their own, in whichever of the 7 languages you're using.
Connect Smart Scale
Simulates pairing with a Bluetooth scale — watch it search, connect, and settle on a reading, which drops straight into the calculator.
Weight & Payout Calculator
Pick a material chip, nudge the weight in 100g steps (or use a preset), and watch your guaranteed cash total update live.
Dealers Tab
Nearby Dealers & Live ETA
Once you request a pickup, a three-stage bar — Confirmed → En Route → Handover — actually advances in real time as your dealer's ETA counts down.
Smart Collection Day
If a dealer runs a fixed weekly round in your pincode, you'll see the next date here before you even ask.
Profile Tab
Digital Scrap Passport
Every completed pickup gets its own record — photo, GPS-verified handover location, weight, grade, and payout — open with View Passport on any past sale.
Environmental Tracker
Trees-equivalent and kilograms of CO₂ prevented, calculated fresh from your actual completed sales — not a placeholder number.
Community & Institutions
Register a college, office, or housing society for scheduled bulk collection, and get linked to the nearest dealer automatically.
Fair Rotation Contract
Lock in one dealer for a 15-day run. When it lapses, renew with the same dealer or switch to another — your call.
How a Pickup Actually Runs
Scan or speak your item
The AI Scanner or Voice Selling fills in material, weight, and grade.
Check the guaranteed price
The payout box totals it up before you commit to anything.
Request Doorstep Pickup
Your real GPS location and photo are attached as handover proof.
Track the dealer live
Watch the progress bar move from Confirmed to En Route to your door.
Mark as Collected
Cash changes hands, and your Digital Scrap Passport unlocks instantly.
Role 2

Kabadiwala

You collect from customers and sell onward to recyclers. This is your trading floor and your route sheet in one.

Commodity Exchange Tab
Live Rates by Category
Metals & Wires, Circuit Boards, Batteries, Glass & Displays, and Plastics — each with day high/low and a real price-trend sparkline that only moves when a recycler actually changes a rate.
Sell Lot → Fair Price Detector
Building a lot manifest, you can propose your own rate — a live badge tells you instantly if it's Fair, Below Benchmark, or above it, before you dispatch.
Warehouse & Recyclers Tab
Distance-Sorted Recyclers
Cards are ranked by your actual real-world distance to each facility, not a fixed list order.
Best Payer Badge
A recycler can publish their own rate for a material (marked ✦) — whoever pays the most for what you're carrying earns the 🏆 Best Payer tag.
Route Optimizer
Orders your pending customer pickups nearest-stop-first and opens the whole run as one multi-stop Google Maps route.
Load Pooling
Two or more completed pickups of the same material can be combined into one wholesale lot for a +5% consolidation bonus.
Smart Collection Day
Set a recurring weekday for your service pincode — it shows up automatically for every customer nearby.
Role 3

Recycler

The compliance end of the chain — verifying weight, catching bad rates, and issuing the paperwork that makes a lot official.

Incoming Lots
Every lot a kabadiwala dispatches to you lands here with its weight, rate, GPS handover point, and CPCB Form-6 manifest number.
Fraud / Underpayment Alert
If a lot's locked-in rate has drifted more than 15% from today's benchmark by the time you go to pay it, you'll get a warning that requires an explicit acknowledgement before you can proceed.
CPCB EPR Certificate
Verifying a lot's weight and confirming payment issues a Form-6 style certificate — collector, material, weight, GPS geotag, and audit status, ready to view or export.
Rate Management
Update the shared platform rate every dealer sees, or publish your own competitive rate for one material to chase the Best Payer badge.

Works the Same for Everyone

A handful of tools sit outside the three role dashboards — reachable from the footer or wherever they're relevant, whichever role you're signed in as.

Ask EcoScrap AI
Type or speak a question — "what's the price of copper wire?", "nearest dealer?" — and get an answer spoken back, pulled from today's real rates and locations, not a script.
E-Waste Hotspot Map
A live map of every registered dealer, recycler, and completed lot. Needs a connection to load its tiles even when the rest of the app is working offline.
Offline Mode
Lose signal and the app keeps showing last-synced prices, with a banner marking anything queued. Reconnect and it syncs itself — nothing to redo.
In-App Connect
A direct chat thread between a kabadiwala and a recycler, attached to whichever facility card you opened it from.

Safety & Hazard Guide

Four real hazards, explained and spoken in all seven languages — open it anytime from the footer.

Never burn insulated wire. Burning PVC releases dioxin gas. Sell cables unstripped — recyclers pay for the plastic too.
Never acid-leach circuit boards at home. It recovers a fraction of the value and the fumes cause serious harm. Sell whole boards to a CPCB unit instead.
Never puncture a lithium battery. Tape the terminals and store in a dry container away from other scrap metal.
Never smash a CRT tube. It implodes with real force, scattering glass and lead dust. Deliver it whole.

Terms Worth Knowing

CPCB / SPCB
Central / State Pollution Control Board — the government body that authorizes e-waste recyclers in India.
EPR
Extended Producer Responsibility — the compliance framework a recycled lot's paperwork is issued under.
Form-6 Manifest
The official record format for an e-waste handover, from collection through to certified recycling.
Kabadiwala
An informal scrap dealer or aggregator — the link between individual customers and licensed recyclers.
Doorstep vs. wholesale rate
Customers sell at the doorstep rate; kabadiwalas resell to recyclers at the (higher) wholesale rate.
Grade A / B / C
The AI Scanner's quality read on an item, worth a 1.1×, 1.0×, or 0.85× payout multiplier respectively.
Best Payer
The badge earned by whichever recycler is currently offering the highest rate for a given material.
